                                                    HOW TO PLAY
====================================================================================================================
|1. Pick a number on the dice from 1 to 6                                                                           |
|2. The dice will be rolled, if the dice lands on the number you chose you lose                                     |
|3. If the dice lands on any other number you win                                                                   |
|4. There is a chance that you will be prompted to do a challenge, which is completely optional to accept           |
|5. If you accept it then the dice will be turn into a coin, you will have to choose between heads and tails        |
|6. If the coin lands on the side you chose, you lose                                                               |
|7. If the coin lands on the opposite side of what you chose, you win                                               |
=====================================================================================================================
